## Framing

For plaques: retain orientation, border, material and raised or engraved motifs, on a neutral stone or garden surface. For urns and boxes: preserve the exact container type and proportions; do not turn a pet ashes casket into a human urn. For pet memorials: preserve species-specific or heart/stone shapes and their motifs.

Make an attractive representative visualisation, not a false exact photograph of completed work. No watermarks, captions, invented brand marks, made-up customer names, or added objects that hide the memorial. If the original depicts a distinctive work, its defining design takes priority over the shared lighting style.

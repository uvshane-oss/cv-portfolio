I can sell. I can lead. I can bring the best out of people — but I'm just as comfortable rolling my sleeves up and doing the job alongside them.

Business is much the same. Find the pain point, think it through and get on with solving it. A good solution doesn't have to cost a million dollars — but it just might generate it.
